Thirteen thousand dollars before day one

A senior engineer on salary runs $18,290 a month fully loaded, $219,480 a year, and the largest early charge lands before any work starts. Recruiting fees, job boards, screening hours, the interview loop: Glassdoor puts that startup bill near $13,500, paid while the seat is still empty. Spread across a $5,995 monthly subscription, the same money covers 2.25 months of delivered development.

Poket Dev starts the ledger at zero. Signup carries no upfront cost, the first request lands on a Trello board within about 24 hours, one card moves at a time, and the code arrives in a private GitHub repo you keep. At the 72 hour turnaround pace the board clears 86 cards across a 260 weekday year, and each card reopens for changes until you sign off.

The gap widens across a full year. The yearly plan prices at $4,995 a month, $59,940 total, 27.3 percent of one loaded senior salary, revisions included and no meetings on the calendar. Ending the subscription ends the spend: cancel anytime and the repository, with everything in it, already sits in your hands.

Pricing and scope sit at Poket Dev, an unlimited software development subscription with nothing due up front.
